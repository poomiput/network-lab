    12 · WAN + eBGP ไป ISP

ip prefix-list DEFAULT-ONLY seq 10 permit 0.0.0.0/0
route-map ISP-IN permit 10
 match ip address prefix-list DEFAULT-ONLY
 set local-preference 200
router bgp 65106
 neighbor 172.31.1.25 remote-as 65001
